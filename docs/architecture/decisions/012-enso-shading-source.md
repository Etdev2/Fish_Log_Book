# 012 — ENSO shading: ONI v5 history + the CPC official outlook, as a static snapshot

**Date:** 2026-10-13 · **Status:** accepted (FL-1, #135)

## Decision
- History: CPC ONI v5 (`https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/data/indices/oni.ascii.txt`). An
  episode is ≥ +0.5 (El Niño) or ≤ −0.5 (La Niña) for ≥ 5 consecutive overlapping seasons
  (CPC's definition). A season is attributed to its centre month.
- Outlook: the CPC Official ENSO Probabilities (9 seasons, issued monthly on the 2nd
  Thursday), copied as published. CPC verifies these against RONI.
- Stored as a static TypeScript snapshot with its retrieval date, regenerated by
  `scripts/refresh-enso.mjs`. There is no runtime fetch and no key.
- Beyond the outlook: **no shading**, labelled "no forecast". ENSO is not predictable years
  out.

## Open (Elliott)
Switch the history to RONI (`RONI.ascii.txt`) to match CPC's current verification index.
