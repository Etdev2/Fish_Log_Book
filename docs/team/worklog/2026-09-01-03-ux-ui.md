### 2026-09-01 | ux-ui | Setup: today's rods and today's locations

Third Fish Log slice. Full write-up in `docs/design/10-setup.md`.

- **Almost none of this was new modelling.** The ontology already had the angler-observed
  current terms (`uphill/downhill/inshore/offshore`), current strength, the structure and
  water vocabularies, and `bottom_depth_m` separate from `depth_fished_m`. Rod setups are
  D21a's `trip_rig` generalised from one standing rig to N slots, not a second table —
  two answers to "what was I fishing with" is the drift ADR 003 exists to prevent. One
  new table: `location_condition`.
- **§10 falls out of the append-only rule.** Re-rigging inserts revision n+1; revision n
  is unwritable (the table revokes UPDATE), so the 8am fish keeps its 40 lb leader.
  Proven against a real PostgreSQL 16 rather than asserted, along with three rods at
  revision 1 — which the old `(trip_id, revision)` constraint made impossible, and which
  is why this migration swaps it for `(trip_id, slot, revision)`.
- **Locations are mutable; the history is the copy.** Editing "West End" at noon corrects
  the present. Catches copied the values, so the morning is untouched, and deleting the
  preset nulls the link and keeps every observed value.
- **A real bug found while building the mark-resolution flow the spec's title promised
  but its body did not contain.** "Say what this was" routed through `logCatch` and
  created a *second* catch, leaving the original mark unresolved forever. Resolving now
  updates the mark in place, preserving its timestamp, trip, position and snapshot.
- **Two UX problems the screenshots caught and the assertions did not:** the species list
  pushed Rod/Where/Save off screen (it now collapses to the answer once picked), and
  "Not recorded" was filled orange — the affirmative colour on the do-nothing option.
  A third came from a failing test: tapping the selected rod deselected it. Preset chips
  are select-only now; "Not recorded" is the explicit clear.
- Checks: verify green (299 tests), build green; 34 Setup browser checks, 11 on mark
  resolution, 10 regression checks on the previous slice since the nav grew to five tabs
  (all five stay hit-testable with the quick mark showing). Not tested on a real phone.
- **Stacked on the Quick Mark PR**, which was still open — that branch's work is the base
  here rather than duplicated.
