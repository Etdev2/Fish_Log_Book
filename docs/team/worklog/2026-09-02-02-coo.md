### 2026-09-02 | coo | ~20m

- The owner answered two of the four questions from this morning's meeting, and both
  answers changed the plan more than the questions suggested. Recorded as rulings in
  `docs/team/meetings/2026-09-02-wednesday-team-meeting.md` §9.
- **No database for now.** Storage stays local — JSON and IndexedDB, no keys, no hosted
  server. The schema gets added later, once the app's shape tells us what we are actually
  storing. A big part of the point is that this app works out on the water with no signal.
- That single decision unblocked nearly everything. Yesterday and this morning, the top of
  the list was "set up the server, then wire sign-in, then write the syncing code," and the
  most valuable feature in the product — recording that you went fishing and caught nothing
  — was sitting behind all of it. It never needed a server. It is ordinary local work and
  it can start now. Three items that said "Blocked" are simply gone.
- Two things get more urgent because of it. Every screen says "Backed up," which was a
  temporary untruth while the syncing code was pending; with no server by choice it is a
  permanent one, so it has to say "Saved on this phone." And with nothing on a server,
  clearing the browser's data wipes every record — so being able to export your log to a
  file stops being a nice extra and becomes the only backup that exists.
- **Web first, then native, both shipping.** What the team wrote up as a fork — web *or*
  native — was a false choice. The owner is doing web now because it can be tested
  one-handed on a real phone without a Mac or Xcode, and native comes after. That also
  quietly fixes the oldest complaint in the worklogs: nobody had ever used this thing on a
  real phone. Now someone will, daily.
- Explained what a "scope freeze" is, since it was jargon and unclear: it is an ordering
  rule, not a ban on ideas — do not start a new area of the app until the half-built ones
  work. The two areas in question were the Tackle Box and Fish Legal. Both are good work.
  Only the ordering was ever in question. The owner has not ruled on it yet.

  work as upcoming when it shipped yesterday.
