  all the tide arithmetic. That works today and stops working the moment we add sun, moon,
  slack countdowns and catch markers on top of it.

  folder that the future iPhone app can be built from; the chart becomes a thing that only
  draws. The look of the chart does not change at all.

  metres and in plain UTC timestamps, and anything shown to the angler in feet or in local
  time is converted at the last moment, in one file.

  predictions, not a measurement, so those values are wrapped in a type that carries the
  reason with them and cannot be printed on screen without it.
