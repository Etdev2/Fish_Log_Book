### 2026-08-31 | ux-ui | tide page single-screen redesign

- The tide page was holding the right information and too much of it at once. Rebuilt it as
  a single-screen instrument: it no longer scrolls, and the chart now gets roughly the
  bottom two thirds of the phone instead of a slice in the middle.
- Scrubbing changed shape. The timeline now slides underneath one fixed orange line down
  the centre of the screen, and the big reading at the top always describes whatever is
  under that line. The old press-and-hold and the separate "selected point" marker are
  gone — there was more than one thing on screen claiming to be "where you are looking",
  and now there is one.
- The header reads out the time under the line rather than always the wall clock. That is a
  reversal of an earlier decision, so it is written up in `docs/design/07-tide-chart.md`
  with the reasoning. To stop it being a quiet lie: a NOW badge shows when the two agree, a
  NOW marker is drawn on the curve when they do not, and a Now button appears in the date
  bar to jump back.
- Everything true-but-rarely-read moved into bottom sheets you open from the thing it
  describes: tap the station name for the NOAA number, datum, position and provenance; the
  moon chip for phase detail; the big reading for the day's highs, lows, slack windows and
  hourly numbers; the date for the cached-day strip.
- Removed from the screen: the feet/metres toggle (it already lives in Settings), the
  prev/next day buttons (arrows in the date bar), the permanent calendar strip, the moon
  card, the chart key, the data footnotes, and the slack diamonds that only ever sat on top
  of the high/low dots. The Next.js dev badge that floated over the bottom nav is off.
- The founder left one call to me: whether the date strip stays visible. It does not — it
  was worth 80–100 pixels of chart, and the date is legible in the control that opens it.
- Checked in a browser at 390x844 and 375x667 (headless Chromium): no page scrolling in
  either, sheets open and close, the Now button and day arrows work, keyboard arrows step
  the read-head 15 minutes, and no console errors. Nobody has held it on a real phone.

### Follow-up the same day — the readout was shaking the screen

- Founder caught it on a real phone: swiping the chart made the whole screen jump. The box
  at the top was growing and shrinking a line at a time as the reading changed, and
  everything below it moved with it.
- Cause: four things in that box appear and disappear as you scrub — the NOW badge, the two
  "Interpolated" provenance markers (which vanish the moment the read-head sits exactly on
  a published NOAA reading), and the slack countdown. Underneath that, a styling rule meant
  to put the marker on its own line was being overridden by Tailwind, so the marker was
  landing inline and costing a full 50px whenever it appeared.
- Fix: every row in that box now reserves the tallest thing it can hold, whether or not
  that thing is currently there. The NOW badge keeps its space and only turns invisible.
  Measured the result in a browser rather than eyeballing it: the box is 184.5px and the
  chart's top edge is 316.5px at all 67 sampled points across the cached window, at
  390x844, 375x667 and 430x932. It was varying by up to 50px before.
- Rule for anyone adding to that box later: reserve the slot, do not add a conditional row.
  Written into `docs/design/07-tide-chart.md`.

### Alignment and consistency pass

Founder asked for a pass against a written alignment/consistency standard before moving on.
Measured every edge in a browser rather than eyeballing it, and fixed what was actually off:

- **Four different left edges** became one. The station name sat at 17px, the card at 14px
  and the big reading at 27px, so nothing lined up down the screen. There are now two
  lines: one for box edges (16px) and one for text (28px), and everything sits on one of
  them. The right-hand side mirrors it — the moon pill, the direction pill, the rate and
  "Tide detail" all end on the same line.
- **The readout became one grid** instead of two independent stacks, so both columns share
  their rows. The time now starts on the same line as the big number below it; it used to
  be pushed sideways by the invisible NOW badge, which is why it looked like it was
  floating.
- **The two "Interpolated" markers** were sitting 10px apart. They now hang on one line.
  This took three attempts: the unit "ft" was inheriting the big number's line spacing and
  dragging the marker down with it.
- **The date is centred over the read-head.** The Now button used to occupy a fourth
  column in that row, which pushed the date 42px off the chart's centre line and left a
  hole when it was hidden. It now floats over the chart it acts on, where appearing and
  disappearing costs no layout at all.
- **One form per fact.** The hour axis said "3 PM" while every other time said "6:04am";
  the chart's day dividers said "27 THU" while the date control said "THU, AUG 27". Both
  now match.
- Deleted two format helpers nothing used any more.
- Re-checked: the readout is still exactly the same height at every scrub position and at
  three phone sizes, so none of this brought the shake back.

Left alone deliberately: the header is ~20px taller for a reader outside the station's time
zone, because their clock's zone label wraps to a second line. It cannot change while
scrubbing, so it does not move anything.
