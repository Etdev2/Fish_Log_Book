### 2026-09-04 | head-dev | 1h

- The founder asked whether pictures beside the identification questions would help, and
  whether to add photos. Yes to pictures — it is the biggest single improvement available
  to that feature. No to photographs, and the reason is worth writing down.
- A photo of a tail comes with water, glare, an angle and somebody's hand. A drawing comes
  with only the thing being asked about. For "spots on both lobes or just the upper one" a
  clean diagram is not the cheap substitute, it is the clearer answer. Photographs would
  also reopen the image-licensing debt we already carry and that photos were cut over.
- The app had already made this decision once: the rockfish screen draws small schematic
  colour swatches and its own comment calls them "the honest 'not a photo' visual". This
  follows that.
- Drew twenty-four little diagrams: the four salmon tail-spot patterns, black and white
  gum lines, the sand bass dorsal-spine tell and the four body patterns, the five whale
  blow shapes, and seven dorsal fin profiles.
- They are drawn in the button's own text colour, so they work selected or not, in either
  theme, and no colour is hard-coded anywhere — which also keeps the design-token rule
  happy without an exception.
- Only the questions that actually decide something got a picture. Size, group size and
  behaviour stayed as plain text; a drawing beside every answer would bury the two that
  matter.
- Redrew the gum-line pair after looking at it on a phone. The first attempt varied the
  line thickness and the two were indistinguishable at that size, which defeats the point.
  Now one is a filled band and the other is hollow.
- A test walks every pack and fails if a question names a drawing that does not exist, or
  if two answers in the same question share one — a typo there would silently render
  nothing and nobody would notice until they were squinting at a fish.
- Checks: tripwires clean, no lint errors, 636 tests, production build green. Looked at
  both screens on a phone-sized window.
- Files: src/components/trait-figure.tsx and its test, packs.ts, fin-id-pack.ts,
  pack-wizard.tsx, fin-id.tsx, identification/types.ts
- Next: the rockfish wizard's own questions are still text-only. They are colour questions,
  so they want real colours rather than the outline style used here.
