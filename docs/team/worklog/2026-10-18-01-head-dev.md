### 2026-10-18 | head-dev (Claude, "Kelp Halyard") | about 15m (15:28–15:43 UTC by the container clock)

Et reviewed the app on an iPhone and asked for a real chart, real fish pictures on every
ID, a rockfish catalog, an ID simple enough for a five-year-old, and pages that do not
lift when scrolled. I wrote that up as a prompt
(`docs/team/handoff/2026-10-18-map-id-photos-prompt.md`) and then worked through it.

**Done**

- The ID screens (salmon, sand bass, rockfish) now show the fish's picture on every
  possible match, always visible. Before you answer anything, every fish is shown so you
  can just pick the one that looks like yours.
- The percentages are gone. Matches now say "Best match", "Could be" or "Less likely".
  Before any answer they say nothing, because four fish at 17% told nobody anything.
  Only the engine's own winner can be called "Best match".
- New rockfish catalog: 19 rockfish, A to Z, each with its picture, a "never keep" warning
  on the no-take ones, and a link to the rules. It is linked from the rockfish ID and the
  Fish Legal home. A test fails if a rockfish is ever added elsewhere and left out.
- iPhone: the page is set not to bounce past its top and bottom, so the tab bar should
  stay on the bottom edge, and the tab bar leaves room for the home-indicator bar. "Add to
  Home Screen" is set up to install a full-screen app with its own icon. Checked in
  desktop Chromium only; not yet tested on a real iPhone (Codex review, 2026-10-18).

**How I checked the pictures**

I looked at every bundled picture the ID screens use (25 of them) and checked each
against the species it is named for. All 25 match, for example the yellow eye on the
yelloweye, the yellow stripe on the china rockfish, the black bars on the treefish, and
pale blotches on kelp bass against dark bars on barred sand bass. 24 of them are
identification drawings from CDFW's marine species portal, not photographs. Most are
signed by Amadeo Bachar; the calico and widow drawings carry other artists' signatures,
though their credit line names Bachar. That credit should be corrected when the photos
are re-sourced. The bronzespotted picture is
a stock photo with no licence, so the ID screens and the catalog leave it out and show
"No checked picture yet" instead.
