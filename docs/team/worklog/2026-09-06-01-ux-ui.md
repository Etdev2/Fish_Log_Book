### 2026-09-06 | ux-ui | 3h

Navigation round. The founder's complaint was that the app does not feel like one place:
some screens have no way back, and guided setup sends you off and abandons you.

- **Fish Legal had no navigation at all.** Its eight pages lived one directory above the
  route group that provides the header and the bottom bar, so tapping "Legal" in the
  bottom bar led to a screen with no bottom bar on it and no back link either. The only
  way out was the browser's own back button. Moved them inside the group; every one of
  them now has the bar, the Menu, and a "Fish Legal" link back to the section home.
  Added a test that fails if any future page is created outside a route group.
- **Back links are one thing now, not five.** The app had "← Passport", "‹ Calendar",
  "Back to the Fish Log", a chevron-and-word, and — on eight screens — nothing. There is
  one `BackLink` component; every screen that has a parent uses it, and the screens that
  had nothing (both notices documents, the trip and spot pages, the Boat Games wizard,
  every Fish Legal page) have one.
- **Setup moved off the calendar onto its own page, at /onboarding.** The calendar is the
  home screen and was being pushed down the page by a tutorial. It now carries a short
  banner instead — a welcome on a fresh install, a "2 of 6 done, continue" line partway
  through, and nothing at all once setup is finished.
- **Finishing a step brings you back.** Every step link is tagged, so the screen it opens
  shows a "Back to setup" strip along the top saying which step you are on and whether it
  has gone green yet. Choosing a region or a tide station in Settings now walks you
  straight back to the checklist with that step ticked — which is the specific thing the
  founder asked for. Steps also land you on the control itself rather than at the top of
  a nine-section Settings page.
- **Setup can be done again.** It is a permanent destination now — in the Menu and in
  Settings — and it has a "Start over" that clears what the guide remembers. That button
  says plainly that it does not un-choose your region or delete your gear, because it
  does not: anything still true today ticks itself straight back.

Checked: `npm run verify` clean (8 lint warnings, all pre-existing in files I did not
touch), `npm run build` clean, and every route that was static before is still static —
the two query-string reads are behind Suspense boundaries so ADR 005 §5 still holds.
Looked at it in a real browser at 390px: walked the whole errand flow, screenshotted the
calendar, the guide, both Settings landings, the Tackle Box errand and a Fish Legal
sub-page.
